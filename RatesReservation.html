Reservation Policies

  • All reservations must be guaranteed with a credit card. We require an advanced deposit for the term of your stay and tax, charged at the time the reservation is made.
  • No rollaways are available for any room.
  • No pets are allowed.
  • Free limited WiFi is available to all rooms.*

Cancellation Policy

We get it — life happens, and sometimes plans don't work out. When that happens, these are our firm policies for all guests and all reservations. Please read them carefully before making a reservation.

7+ Days Before Arrival

Refundable, subject to a processing fee — or convert your deposit to a gift certificate for future use at no additional fee.

2–7 Days Before Arrival

Non-refundable. Can only be converted to a gift certificate at half the value of the original deposit.

Within 2 Days / No-Show

No refund. No-show reservations and early departures also do not receive any refund, regardless of the reason.

All refunds are subject to a 6% processing fee, regardless of why your plans changed or current factors impacting travel on the Big Sur coast. Any credit card changes to your deposit or stay will also result in a 6% processing fee.

Keep in mind that we cannot guarantee road or weather conditions — including fog, rain, hail, lack of streetlights, falling rocks, lightning strikes, animals on the highway, lack of GPS signal, or lack of cell service.

"Thank you for your understanding. We are just a small, family-operated business — not a mega-corporation with thousands of properties. Please try to be as kind to our small staff as we try to be to our guests and visitors."

Good to Know

Check-In & Check-Out

Check-In

Begins at 4pm. If a room is vacant and clean prior to 4pm, we're happy to check you in early.

Check-Out

11am. Late check-outs up to 3pm are charged a half-day rate; after 3pm, a full-day rate applies.

In-Room Comforts

Rooms include down comforters, but do not have telephones or televisions.

Occupancy

All rooms accommodate up to 2 people.

Protecting This Coast

When Visiting Big Sur

The following rules are in effect throughout Big Sur, both for your own safety and to protect the plants and animals that live here.

🐾

Wildlife Protection

Harassment or disturbance of wild animals is prohibited by federal law.

🏙

Leave No Trace

Collecting shells, rocks, wood, plants, or animals is not allowed. All features of this reserve are protected by law.

🔥

Fire & Smoking

Smoking is not permitted in buildings. Fires of all types are prohibited.
