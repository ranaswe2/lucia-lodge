Getting Here

Directions to Lucia Lodge

Lucia Lodge sits on the ocean side of Highway 1, about an hour south of Carmel, or 45–60 minutes north of San Simeon and Cambria (depending on road conditions).

The lodge is visible from the road. We offer self check-in — simply drive down the driveway and park to access your room.

You'll Know It When You See It

Look for the Lucia Sign

A wooden sign marks the turnoff right at the "Lucia" street sign on Highway 1 — you'll see it well before you arrive. Pull off into the driveway and park; there's no front desk to check in at, just your room waiting.

Distances

From the North or South

From the North

From Monterey/Carmel, take Highway 1 south to Lucia.

From the South

From San Luis Obispo/Morro Bay, take Highway 1 north to Lucia.

For current road conditions on Highway 1, check the California Department of Transportation website — enter "1" in the box marked "Enter Highway Number." Visit CalTrans QuickMap →
