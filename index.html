Accommodations

Ten Rooms, 300 Feet Above the Ocean

Lucia Lodge offers ten updated cabins and rooms isolated on a cliff 300 feet above the ocean. Guests enjoy a view overlooking a natural bay extending to a breathtaking vista of the south coast. Some rooms face landward, taking in the striking Santa Lucia Mountains — named after St. Lucy by Spanish explorer Sebastian Vizcaino in 1602.

The accommodations are rustic in nature and offer neither telephones nor television. Rather, guests are treated to a romantic setting amid ever-changing seascapes and views of majestic mountains. It is not uncommon to see passing whales, sea otters playing in giant kelp beds, and soaring birds. The ever-present sound of breaking waves is both exhilarating and, at the same time, relaxing.

A sitting area at the edge of the cliff offers all guests a splendid view of the ocean directly below, with a picnic area located just below the cabins at the edge of the cliff.

Rooms are rustic and unplugged by design — no phones or TVs. Free WiFi and a complimentary grab-and-go breakfast are available to every guest each morning.

Room 10

The Honeymoon Cottage

The cabin closest to the edge of the cliff is our largest accommodation, with bay windows looking right over the water. Built by the Harlan family in the 1930s, it carries a gas fireplace and a four-poster queen bed — the most spectacular ocean view on the property, with a historic feel throughout.

Rooms 7, 8 & 9

Oceanview Cliffside Cabins

Four freestanding cabins sit near the edge of the cliffs — the original accommodations built by the Harlan family in the 1930s. Each has a gas fireplace and a four-poster queen bed, offering some of the most spectacular ocean views on the entire coast, with a distinctly historic feel.

Rooms 1, 2, 3 & 4

Partial Ocean View Rooms

At the other end of the lodge, four standard rooms sit closer to the mountains, each with a pillow-top queen bed and hypoallergenic comforter. Rooms 1–3 also enjoy an ocean view, set back a bit further from the cliff edge.

Rooms 5 & 6

Mountain View Rooms

At the mid-point of the lodge are our two most modern rooms — larger, with a pillow-top queen bed and hypoallergenic comforter. These rooms don't face the ocean, but offer a spectacular view of the Santa Lucia Mountains. A sofa-bed option is available for a third guest.

Our History

Five Generations on One Piece of Coast

The Harlan family has stewarded this stretch of the Big Sur coast since it was wilderness — through the arrival of Highway 1, decades of quiet hospitality, and one recent loss they're still rebuilding from.

1800s

The Harlan Homestead

The land is homesteaded by the pioneering Harlan family, among the first to settle this remote stretch of the Big Sur South Coast.

1937

The Lucia Store Opens

The Lucia store opens its doors the same year Highway 1 is completed, quickly becoming a waypoint for mail, groceries, and coffee for travelers and locals alike.

Mid-1900s – Today

A Lodge Grows on the Cliffs

The lodge expands along the ocean-side cliffs, earning a reputation for having some of the best unobstructed views on the entire coast.

2021

Fire, and a Family's Resolve

The original restaurant and store are lost to fire. The lodge, 100 yards north, remains open — and in the same pioneering spirit as their ancestors, the Harlan family begins planning to rebuild.

Today

The Fifth Generation

The fifth generation of the Harlan family welcomes guests to Lucia, a village named for the metamorphic mountains that surround it — still perched above the same surf their ancestors once looked out on.
