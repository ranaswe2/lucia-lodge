Accommodations

Ten Rooms, 300 Feet Above the Ocean

Lucia Lodge offers ten updated cabins and rooms isolated on a cliff 300 feet above the ocean. Guests enjoy a view overlooking a natural bay extending to a breathtaking vista of the south coast. Some rooms face landward, taking in the striking Santa Lucia Mountains — named after St. Lucy by Spanish explorer Sebastian Vizcaino in 1602.

The accommodations are rustic in nature and offer neither telephones nor television. Rather, guests are treated to a romantic setting amid ever-changing seascapes and views of majestic mountains. It is not uncommon to see passing whales, sea otters playing in giant kelp beds, and soaring birds. The ever-present sound of breaking waves is both exhilarating and, at the same time, relaxing.

A sitting area at the edge of the cliff offers all guests a splendid view of the ocean directly below, with a picnic area located just below the cabins at the edge of the cliff.

Rooms are rustic and unplugged by design — no phones or TVs. Free WiFi and a complimentary grab-and-go breakfast are available to every guest each morning.

Where You'll Stay

A Quick Guide to the Property Layout

Lucia Lodge is arranged in three groupings along the cliff, from the ocean's edge inland toward the mountains.

Rooms 7–10

The Cliffside Cabins

Four freestanding cabins built by the Harlan family in the 1930s, right at the cliff's edge with the widest ocean views. Room 10 is the honeymoon cottage.

Rooms 1–4

Standard Rooms

Set back further from the cliff, closer to the mountains. Rooms 1–3 still enjoy an ocean view.

Rooms 5–6

Modern Rooms

Located at the mid-point of the lodge, these are our largest and newest rooms, facing the Santa Lucia Mountains.
